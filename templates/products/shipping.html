Order Processing

  • 1 Order Confirmed — You'll receive an email confirmation immediately after placing your order.
  • 2 Payment Verified — Orders are processed once payment is successfully confirmed, typically within a few minutes.
  • 3 Packed & Dispatched — Your order is carefully packed and dispatched within 1–2 business days from our Dublin warehouse.
  • 4 Dispatched — Your order is shipped. Tracking is provided for orders above a certain value or upon request. Standard orders may be sent via untracked post.
  • 5 Delivered — Your instruments arrive safely packaged to ensure they reach you in perfect condition.

Order Tracking

Tracked delivery is optional and available as an add-on at checkout. All standard orders are shipped via untracked post at no charge.

You can add express tracked delivery at checkout — the price varies by country and is shown automatically when you select your country. Once dispatched, you will receive a tracking number via email to monitor your delivery via An Post.

If you have not received your order within the estimated delivery window, please contact us and we will investigate promptly.

Customs & Import Duties

For deliveries outside the European Union, your order may be subject to customs duties, import taxes, or fees imposed by your country's customs authority. These charges are the responsibility of the recipient and are not covered by MAK Supplies.

We recommend checking with your local customs office for more information before placing your order. MAK Supplies is not responsible for any delays caused by customs processing.

Damaged or Lost Shipments

In the unlikely event that your order arrives damaged or is lost in transit, please contact us within 7 days of the expected delivery date.

We will work with the courier to resolve the issue promptly and arrange a replacement or full refund as appropriate. Please retain all original packaging and take photographs of any damage, as these may be required for a courier claim.
