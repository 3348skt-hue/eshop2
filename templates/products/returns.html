Our Returns Policy

At MAK Supplies, we take great pride in the quality of our surgical and medical instruments. We want you to be completely satisfied with every purchase. If for any reason you are not happy with your order, we offer a clear and fair returns process in accordance with EU consumer rights regulations.

You have 14 days from the date of delivery to request a return. After this period, we are unable to accept return requests unless the item is faulty or damaged.

Return Conditions

To be eligible for a return, items must meet the following conditions:

Item is unused and in its original condition
All original packaging is intact and undamaged
Item is unopened and factory sealed
Return requested within 14 days of delivery
Sterile or single-use items cannot be returned once opened
Items showing signs of use or wear are not eligible
Items with damaged or missing original packaging
Custom or specially ordered items

Non-Returnable Items

Due to the nature of medical and surgical instruments, the following categories are strictly non-returnable for health and safety reasons:

Sterile instruments — Any item that has been removed from sterile packaging cannot be returned under any circumstances, as sterility cannot be guaranteed once the seal is broken.

This policy is in place to protect the health and safety of all our customers and complies with applicable medical device regulations in Ireland and the EU.

Return Shipping

Customers are responsible for the cost of return shipping unless the item received was incorrect or arrived damaged/defective.

We strongly recommend using a tracked and insured shipping service when returning items. MAK Supplies cannot be held responsible for items lost or damaged in return transit.

If your item arrived damaged or you received an incorrect product, we will arrange and cover the cost of the return collection. Please contact us immediately in this case.

How to Return an Item

  • 1 Contact Us — Email us at 3348skt@gmail.com or use our Contact Form within 14 days of receiving your order. Include your order number and reason for return.
  • 2 Await Approval — Our team will review your return request and respond within 2 business days with instructions and our return address.
  • 3 Pack Securely — Carefully repack the item in its original packaging. Include a note with your name, order number, and reason for return inside the parcel.
  • 4 Ship the Item — Send the parcel using a tracked courier service and share the tracking number with us via email.
  • 5 Refund Processed — Once we receive and inspect the returned item, we will process your refund within 5–7 business days to your original payment method.

Refunds

Once your return is received and inspected, we will notify you of the approval or rejection of your refund by email.

If approved, your refund will be processed to your original payment method within 5–7 business days. Please allow additional time for your bank or card provider to process the credit.

Please note that original shipping costs, if any were charged, are non-refundable unless the return is due to an error on our part or a defective product.

If you haven't received your refund within 10 business days of approval, please check with your bank first, then contact us and we will investigate.

Faulty or Incorrect Items

If you receive an item that is faulty, damaged, or different from what you ordered, please contact us within 7 days of delivery. In these cases, we will:

Arrange a free return collection, provide a full replacement at no extra cost, or issue a full refund — whichever you prefer.

Please take photographs of the damaged or incorrect item and include them in your email to help us resolve your case as quickly as possible.
